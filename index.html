Warp to Weft

How a length of cloth becomes a suit.

Four hands touch every piece before it reaches yours — this is the order they work in.

Dye the base

Cotton is soaked in natural indigo and madder vats for up to six hours, then sun-dried on the roof.

Block print

Hand-carved teak blocks stamp the ajrak motifs, layer by layer, each color needing its own block.

Hand embroider

Chikankari and gota artisans work the panels on a wooden frame, one needle pass at a time.

Cut & stitch

Our tailors in Lahore cut to your measurements and finish every seam with a French seam inside.
